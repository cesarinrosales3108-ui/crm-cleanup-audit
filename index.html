Questions

Is my data safe?

I never connect to your live CRM. You upload a CSV export and I work on that file. The audit runs locally, nothing is uploaded to a third party, and you can ask for the file to be deleted when we're done.

What if my CSV has messy column names?

That's normal and it's handled. Email, Correo, work_email and E-mail Address all map to the same field. Same for phone, company, and last-activity date in English or Spanish.

How is 23.8% duplicates a real number, not a guess?

It's from a 500-record export built to reproduce a CRM that hasn't been governed for a few years. 117 duplicates were exact matches on email or phone; 2 were fuzzy matches on name plus company. Those 2 are the ones that matter — a spreadsheet never catches them.

Why won't it just delete the duplicates?

Because a shared office phone or a reassigned mobile is not proof that two records are the same person. During testing, phone-only matching merged two different customers. It now needs two independent signals before it merges anything, and everything goes to a review plan first.

What does the cleanup cost if I go ahead?

Audit and plan: $3,000. Full cleanup: $12,000. Ongoing monthly hygiene: $5,000/mo. Those come from the economic model, not a package sheet — if your data is in better shape, the price is lower.
