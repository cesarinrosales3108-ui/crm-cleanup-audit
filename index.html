What you actually get

A report on your own data from an engine built for exactly this. Not a checklist, not a template.

Duplicate records resolved by entity, not by string

"John Smith", "J. Smith" and "Jon Smyth" at one company resolve to a single person. Exact-match dedupe catches none of them — on a 500-record sample it caught 117 of 119.

Empty fields recovered, not lost

When a duplicate holds a value the other one is missing, that value is recovered. 92 blanks filled on the sample run. Merging without this step quietly destroys data.

Forgotten leads, separated from dead ones

Contacts with no activity in 90+ days, split from records that were already closed or never viable. That distinction is the difference between a list you can work and one you delete.

Questions

Is my data safe?

I never connect to your live CRM. You send a CSV export and I work on that file. It's used for your audit and deleted on request. I'd rather lose the data than your trust — that's the whole basis of this service.

What if my CSV has messy column names?

That's normal and it's handled. Email, Correo, work_email and E-mail Address all map to the same field, as do phone, company and last-activity date in English or Spanish.

How is 23.8% duplicates a real number?

From the 500-record export shown above. 117 duplicates were exact matches on email or phone; 2 were fuzzy matches on name plus company. Those 2 are the interesting ones — a spreadsheet never catches them, and they're usually the records causing double-follow-up.

Why won't it just delete the duplicates?

Because a shared office line or a reassigned mobile isn't proof that two records are the same person. Phone-only matching merged two different customers during testing. It now needs two independent signals before merging anything, and everything lands in a review plan first.

What does the cleanup cost?

Audit and plan: $3,000. Full cleanup: $12,000. Ongoing monthly hygiene: $5,000/mo. Those come from the economic model on your data, not a package sheet — if your CRM is in better shape, the price is lower.

I'm an agency. Can I resell this?

Yes — white-label, fixed price per sprint, you keep the margin and I work under your brand. I already delivered the $15,657 report format to two HubSpot partners.
