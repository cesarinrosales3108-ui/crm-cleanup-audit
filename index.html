FAQ

Questions agencies ask first.

Will you ever connect to my live CRM?

No. Everything runs on an export you send me. That is deliberate: it means a bug on my side cannot damage your data, and you can stop at any point without unwinding anything.

What happens to duplicate records?

Nothing happens without your approval. I produce a merge plan where every candidate pair has a score and the evidence behind it. Records that look similar but are not certain go to a manual review list, never to automatic deletion.

Why are similar records not merged automatically?

Because a wrong merge deletes a real contact, and you find out later. Two people can share a surname, a company and an email address. I only merge automatically when the name of record matches too; everything else is flagged with its score for you to decide.

Is the cost estimate exact?

The counts are exact, because they come from your file. The dollar figures are a model on assumptions the report lists explicitly. If your cost per rep or your average deal size differ from the defaults, change the assumption and the number moves with it.

How do you price a large database?

By scope, not per record. A 50,000-row HubSpot with a Salesforce migration history is a different job from a 3,000-row export with simple duplicates, and it is priced that way. I send the scope in writing before starting.

Do you work under other agencies' brands?

Yes. Reports and cleaned exports carry your name, and I do not contact your clients. If you would rather handle your client directly and use me only for the cleanup, that works too.
