FAQ

Questions

Is my data safe?

I never connect to your live CRM. You send a CSV export, I work on that file, and it's deleted on request after the report. I'd rather lose the data than your trust.

What exactly is in the report?

A PDF with: records analyzed, duplicates found and resolved, empty fields recovered, stale records flagged, invalid emails with the reason each one fails, and the economic model with every assumption listed so you can change them.

Will you delete anything from my CRM?

No. I never connect to your CRM, so I can't. Everything I produce is a proposal — including the merge list, where each pair comes with the score that justified it and which record survives.

What if my file is huge?

Up to 50,000 rows is included. Above that I'll tell you the real price before doing anything, rather than discovering it halfway through.

Is the number real or an estimate?

Both, and the report says which is which. The duplicate counts come from your file and are exact. The dollar figures are a model built on assumptions you can see and change — your cost per rep, your conversion rate, your average deal size. If you disagree with an assumption, we change it and the number moves.
